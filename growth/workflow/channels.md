## Choosing actions

- For search channels, consider demand signals, page quality, and product relevance together. High impressions with zero clicks are a clue to investigate, not proof of a title problem.
- Content should fully serve the reader's task. The reason to continue into the product should be a real follow-up need, not a withheld answer.
- Check store or landing-page promises against the actual experience before conversion experiments. Verify platform mechanisms and field limits at execution time rather than treating them as permanent template facts.
- With limited traffic, documented manual observations or user conversations can help explain problems. Contact others only within the user's actual authorization.
- Existing payment or transaction signals can be investigated directly without validating every other funnel step first.
- Correct factual errors, misleading promises, and experience failures promptly. Record their effect on experiments instead of waiting for new traffic data or the experiment's end.
